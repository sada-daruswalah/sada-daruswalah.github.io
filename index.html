About Us

The voice and spirit of our students

SADA — the Students Association of Daruswalah Academy — is the official student body of the Academy. It brings every student together under one banner to learn, lead and serve, guided by the values of faith, knowledge and good character.

Through its wings, SADA organises academic, literary, cultural and spiritual programmes throughout the year. It manages the daily assembly, supports the Academy library, and gives every student a place to discover and grow their talents.

This portal is the digital home of SADA, bringing the Association's records and services together in one place.

One body, every student

From the first class to the final year, every student of the Academy belongs to SADA and has a voice in it.

Led by students

Office bearers chosen from among the students plan and run each programme, guided by the Academy faculty.

Words of the Chairman

“

Assalamu Alaikum, dear friends.

It is a great honour to serve as the Chairman of the Students Association of Daruswalah Academy. SADA is not just an organisation; it is a family of students who share one dream: to learn with sincerity, to live with discipline and to serve with a smile.

Every one of us carries a special talent. SADA exists to discover that talent, to give it a stage and to help it shine. Whether in the classroom, on the stage, in the library or in the assembly ground, each small effort we make together builds a stronger Academy and a brighter future.

With this digital portal, we take a new step forward. Our records, our attendance and our library are now organised and open to every member. I invite every student to take part, to stay active and to make SADA proud.

Our Wings

How SADA works

The Association carries out its programmes through dedicated wings, each led by student office bearers.

Academic Wing

Study circles, quizzes, revision support and recognition of academic excellence.

Literary & Arts Wing

Speeches, essays, poetry, calligraphy and preparation for arts festivals.

Spiritual Wing

Daily prayers, dhikr gatherings, moral classes and programmes on special occasions.

Library & Reading Wing

Runs the Digital Library, reading campaigns and book reviews.

Discipline & Assembly Wing

Conducts the morning assembly and keeps attendance records.

Sports & Welfare Wing

Games, health awareness and care for the wellbeing of every student.
